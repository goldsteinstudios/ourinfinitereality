    The Chapters¶

    Enter anywhere. Each one opens onto the whole.

    Core Structure¶

    The chapters that establish the coordinate system:

    • Chapter 1 — The Coordinate System Where everything begins. The two axes. The paradoxical center.

    • Chapter 11 — Void and Function The wheel, the pot, the room. Function IS the emptiness.

    • Chapter 25 — Spherical Recursion ✓ Guodian The full recursive cycle, explicitly stated. 大→逝→遠→反.

    • Chapter 42 — Dimensional Proof 道生一，一生二，二生三，三生萬物. How dimensions emerge.

    Pattern Dynamics¶

    How the pattern moves:

    • Chapter 40 — The Oscillation Engine ✓ Guodian 反者道之動. Reversal is the movement of pattern.

    • Chapter 2 — Co-emergence ✓ Guodian 有無相生. How opposites arise together.

    • Chapter 16 — Return to Root ✓ Guodian 歸根曰靜. The 知常 cascade.

    • Chapter 22 — The Arc Operation 曲則全. Curved, then complete.

    Observation & Frame¶

    Different ways of seeing:

    • Chapter 41 — Frame Responses ✓ Guodian 上士聞道. How different frames respond to pattern.

    • Chapter 52 — Return to Source ✓ Guodian 天下有始. The mother and the children.

    • Chapter 56 — Mysterious Sameness ✓ Guodian 知者不言. Knowing and speaking as orthogonal operations.

    Alignment (德)¶

    Straight path through internal state space:

    • Chapter 38 — Alignment Degradation 上德不德. How alignment degrades through layers.

    • Chapter 51 — Pattern Nurtures 道生之，德畜之. Pattern generates; alignment nurtures.

    • Chapter 21 — Pattern-Alignment 孔德之容. The shape of profound alignment.

    Water & Yielding¶

    The paradigm of aligned action:

    • Chapter 8 — Water 上善若水. The highest good is like water.

    • Chapter 78 — Water Overcomes 天下莫柔弱於水. Nothing softer overcomes harder.

    • Chapter 76 — Soft and Hard 人之生也柔弱. The living are soft; the dead are hard.
