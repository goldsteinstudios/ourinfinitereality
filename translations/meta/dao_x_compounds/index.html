    道X Compounds¶

    "The Pattern of X" — Section Headers in the Dao De Jing¶

    The Discovery¶

    When the text says 道X, it means: "X is an aspect of 道, and here's how the 道X works."

    Each 道X compound functions as a section header introducing a domain and its 為 (wéi) — its operation, mechanics, active working.

    The 為 Pattern¶

    Each 道X introduces an aspect, then documents its 為 — what it does, how it operates:

    Compound 為 (Operation)
    道大 Overflows → extends → wraps around (sphere proof)
    道常 Non-acts yet completes everything
    道沖 Used without filling
    道之動 Wraparound (continuous extension arriving at origin)
    道之用 Yielding
    道生 Generates 一→二→三→萬物

    The Grammar¶

    道X = "Here is aspect X of pattern. Here is how it works:"

    This means: - 道大 isn't "the Dao is great" — it's "The pattern of infinity: [proof follows]" - 道德 isn't "the Way and Virtue" — it's "The pattern of alignment: [dynamics follow]" - 道常 isn't "the constant Dao" — it's "The pattern of implicitness: [operation follows]"

    The text is organized as aspect introductions followed by mechanics.

    Implications for Translation¶

    When you encounter 道X: 1. Recognize it as a compound (section header) 2. Read what follows as "how 道X works" 3. Don't separate into "the Dao" + adjective

    When you encounter X道: 1. May be different construction (e.g., 天之道 = "heaven's pattern") 2. Check if possessive or compound
