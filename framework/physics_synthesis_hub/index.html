    Not metaphorically. Structurally. Energy is the turning required to preserve paradox—the impossible center that cannot be occupied but must be circulated around for anything to exist.

    Physical Domain RSM Interpretation

    Black holes What happens when rotation fails
    Quantum mechanics What rotation looks like before angle selection
    Mass How rotation manifests as form

    The five synthesis documents in this series demonstrate that these are not separate phenomena requiring separate explanations. They are structural consequences of the same geometric necessity: reality cannot occupy its own center, so it turns.

    Falsification Conditions¶

    The RSM physics framework is falsifiable. It would be disproven if:

    1. Scale termination discovered — Finding a smallest indivisible unit would violate infinite divisibility
    2. E ∝ 1/r violated — If precision becomes "free" (no energy cost for finer measurement)
    3. Accuracy becomes frame-dependent — If the Y₁·X₁ = k relationship varies between frames
    4. Strictly localized, non-field information found — Contradicts gradient structure
    5. Conservation without symmetry — Violates Noether correspondence
    6. Causality without co-emergence — Field equations require → not ⟺
    7. Entropy reaches final maximum — Paradox cannot be exhausted; "heat death" is structurally impossible
