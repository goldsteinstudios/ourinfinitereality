    From conversations (lines 49071-49099, 06_formalism.md):

    e is the constant that allows multiplicative processes to preserve proportion under infinite divisibility.

    • If you zoom into any point on the curve X = 1/Y, the slope (the rate of change of one axis relative to the other) must still preserve the reciprocal relationship.
