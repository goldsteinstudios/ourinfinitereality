Nested Wheels

A wheel rolls down a road. On the end of one spoke sits a smaller wheel, its axle pointing along that spoke. On the end of one of its spokes sits a smaller one again, and so on, as deep as you like. Every level is built by the same rule: the parent’s axle becomes one of the child’s in-plane directions, and the spoke becomes the child’s axle. Nothing new is added on the way down.

Turn it into a spinning top under two pulls, hang a child on every spoke to grow a tree, tilt the children off their spokes, and watch where the deepest point goes. The presets are regimes found by scanning the size ratio λ, the spin ratio ρ and the depth.
