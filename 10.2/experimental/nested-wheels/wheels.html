Capture, save & record

A PNG snapshot stores every slider, camera and phase inside the file; load it back with “load PNG or JSON”. Recording captures the canvas as it plays; “record one run” resets, plays through all revolutions, then saves.

keys: space play · r reset · ← → step · f fullscreen · c snapshot · v record · esc stop

Parameter sweep — animate a slider across the run

The slider moves linearly from “from” to “to” as θ₀ runs 0 → the full run. Combine with “record one run” to film it.

Drive — rolling wheel, or spinning top under two pulls

Sun at +90° is straight overhead, exactly opposite gravity. Precession per turn of spin scales as 1/ω² — faster spin, slower wobble.

Children — one per wheel, or one on every spoke

With one child on every spoke the structure is a tree: 8 spokes at depth 3 is 585 wheels. Depth is capped so the tree stays under ~700 wheels.

Child orientation — where each child's axis points

α = 0 is the nail: axis along the spoke. α = 90°, β = 0 turns the axis along the parent's tangent — the child circle then contains the parent axle and sweeps a torus. β = 90° instead lays it flat in the parent's plane (coplanar epicycle).
