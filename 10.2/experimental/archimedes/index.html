Archimedes’ Method

Around 250 BCE Archimedes trapped π between two polygons: one drawn inside the circle, one drawn around it. Add sides and the inner perimeter climbs while the outer one falls. They close in on π from both sides, and neither ever lands on it.

Every polygon here is exactly right about itself. The hexagon’s ratio is 3, and that’s the correct answer for a hexagon. None of them is the circle. Press play, or step through by hand.
