Glossary

R\mathcal{R}R · Unrestricted Reality
Reality with no final outside and no final inside: infinitely vast and infinitely divisible. The one premise, and it is conditional.

000 and 111 · The “no” and the “thing”
Split the word nothing down the middle: the no (000) and the thing (111). Neither obtains alone; they are coparturient.
