0. Epistemological Scope: The Language Is Not the Thing

  1. Register Non-Identity: Physics and mathematics are not reality itself; they are frame-bound languages invented to describe reality. Any explicit definition, coordinate chart, or symbolic marker is structurally non-identical to the invariant relational reality it points to.

Premise 1: Unrestricted Reality (R\mathcal{R}R)

  • Scope Guardrail (R\mathcal{R}R vs. R1R_1R1​): R\mathcal{R}R names unrestricted Reality-scope. Split the ordinary English word “nothing” down the middle into its two halves:
