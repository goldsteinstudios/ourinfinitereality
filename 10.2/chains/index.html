The Chains

Each chain is written to stand alone, so they're presented separately. The structural chain uses no ancient text, and the mathematics needs no Chinese. The interesting thing happens when you start to read them together. The map ties their concepts together.

Current set: RSM v10.2. Not sealed.

  1. 01 The Structural Chain

    The model worked out step by step, in plain language. No ancient texts, no units, no calculus.
  2. 02 Mathematical Construction

    The same structure in coordinates: the curve, the balance line, the recursion, and the proofs.
  3. 03 Laozi Correspondence & Guodian Visual Paleography

    The Guodian bamboo slips, the Mawangdui silk manuscripts and the received Daodejing, read alongside the model.
  4. 04 Looking at a Tree (and a Wheel, a Pot, and a Room)

    One tree, looked at closely: cambium, rings, rays, branches, and the pith at the center.
  5. 05 Claims, Decisions, and Open Questions

    Every term defined, the decisions made so far, and the questions still open.
  6. 06 Suggested Implications, Physical Extrapolations, and Tests

    Where the model might be tested, and what it suggests in physics, biology and history.

Written by Claude in conversation with Will Goldstein.
