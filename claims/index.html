What RSM Claims (And Doesn't)

A boundary-setting document

The Core Claim

RSM claims one thing: if reality is infinitely vast and infinitely divisible, then a single structural pattern follows necessarily — stable structures persist by circulating around a crossing their frame never meets, and are generated at a crossing that is met but can never become a frame of its own. The claim is conditional; the derivation is what's checkable; the renderings into physics and biology are correspondences that carry no evidential weight of their own. [candidate]

The Dao De Jing is deliberately not on that list. The framework grew out of the text, so their agreement is not evidence of anything. The DDJ is the subject of translation here.

That's it. That's the claim. Everything below is a way of being careful about it — what's an empirical prediction you could test, what's an interesting analogy, and what the framework deliberately says nothing about.

What RSM Predicts (Empirically Testable)

This is the framework's only evidential section. Every other correspondence on this site carries zero evidential weight. Each prediction requires preregistration before any check counts.

Prediction Status
Cross-frame unit relations, if closed, take power-law form between nesting levels — operationalized through measured RG critical exponents and/or the holographic area law. [falsifiable — target]
The framework must derive the exponent-form before the check. Currently a target.
Structural recession gives a signature distinguishable from ΛCDM. [falsifiable — unbuilt]
The distinguishing observational signature is the explicit open task. Unbuilt.
Unitarity holds. Objective-collapse theories violate it by design, are empirically live, and are increasingly constrained; their confirmation would strike the floor's dynamical face directly. [falsifiable]
A genuine kill-condition.

Earlier versions carried further predictions. They are not restated here pending re-verification against source — the current chain declines to reproduce them from memory, and so does this page.

What RSM Suggests (Structural Analogies)

These are pattern recognitions. Interesting, possibly insightful, but not derivations or proofs.

  • - DDJ Chapter 1 reads as a coordinate system
  • - DDJ Chapter 11 encodes O1 geometry
  • - Euler's identity is "Contrast, Rotation, Closure" in five symbols
  • - Zero is better understood as "generative" than "empty"
  • - Hurricane eyes, tree pith, and atomic nuclei share O1 structure

Analogies invite investigation. They don't constitute evidence.

What RSM Does NOT Claim

These are explicitly outside the framework's scope. Not "not yet addressed" - deliberately excluded.

Topic RSM Position
Consciousness No claim. Pattern recognition doesn't explain experience.
Physics Rendering only — every entry is a correspondence ("maps to," never "is") with zero evidential weight, except the preregistered predictions above. RSM does not derive ℏ, the ½, or Fourier conjugacy.
Metaphysics No claim. RSM describes structure, not whether things "really exist."
Cosmology No claim. Whether the universe "requires" this structure is unknown.
Ethics No claim. Description, not prescription. How to live is your business.
Theology No claim. "Generative center" is not God, Tao, or Brahman.
Ancient author intent No claim. Parallel patterns don't mean Laozi "knew" this.

Quick Reference

"Does RSM claim X?"

  1. Is X the core pattern itself (centers, gradients, boundaries, recursion)? Claimed.
  2. Is X an empirical mapping (plant biology, Kleiber, etc.)? Predicted, testable.
  3. Is X a structural analogy (DDJ readings, Euler interpretation)? Suggested, not proven.
  4. Is X about consciousness, physics fundamentals, metaphysics, or ethics? Not claimed.

The one-liner: RSM describes one pattern of persistence and tries to be honest about which of its claims are math, which are empirical tests, and which are interesting analogies.

Pattern, not proof. Description, not prescription.
